## Automatic motion

The active gallery runs a capped 25 fps canvas scene, with DPR capped at 1.5. A photographic mesh gently lowers the cigar hand and lifts it again on a 15-second cycle, with a small breathing movement. The ember brightens during the draw; exhaled smoke follows, while independent smoke plumes and rising wisps continue between draws. Three particle depths and asynchronous lighting add environmental life. Motion requires no hover, pointer or click.

Inactive galleries and hidden browser documents suspend rendering. Global Pause freezes this scene. Reduced-motion users retain the photographic room and static character, with ambient effects hidden. Mobile uses fewer particles and a smaller mesh.
